How it works

Type a name for each player and pick the number of rounds. Every player is on a court in every round, four to a court, two against two.

Partners are always new. Opponents repeat only when the player count makes repeats unavoidable. Shuffle Again gives a different draw. Print makes a copy for the front desk.
